Introduction

Jollof rice is a beloved one-pot dish across West Africa, especially Nigeria, Ghana, and Senegal. Long-grain parboiled rice is cooked in a rich sauce of tomatoes, peppers, onions, and spices until every grain is tender and full of flavor. It is a staple at parties, weddings, and family meals, and this version serves about six people.

Ingredients

the following are all the list of ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled Rice
  • 6 large ripe tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (ata rodo), adjusted to your heat tolerance
  • 2 medium onions (one blended, one sliced)
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 stock cubes
  • Salt to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the rice in warm water until the water runs mostly clear, then drain and set aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnets, and one onion until smooth.
  3. Pour the blended mixture into a pot and boil on medium heat until the water reduces by about half.
  4. Heat the oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot. Fry the sliced onion until soft, then add the tomato paste and fry for 3 to 5 minutes.
  5. Add the reduced pepper mixture and fry, stirring often, until the sauce thickens and the oil rises to the surface.
  6. Stir in the curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, stock cubes, and salt.
  7. Pour in the stock and bring it to a boil.
  8. Add the washed rice and stir well so the rice is fully coated in sauce.
  9. Cover the pot tightly (foil under the lid helps), then reduce the heat to low.
  10. Cook for 30 to 40 minutes, stirring gently once or twice, until the rice is tender.
  11. Turn off the heat, let it rest for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and serve.

Safety Notes

  • Important: Handle the scotch bonnet peppers carefully.They are extremely hot, so wash your hands thoroughly after touching them and avoid touching your eyes or face.
  • Warning: Hot oil can splatter and cause burns.Add ingredients slowly and keep a safe distance from the pot.
  • Important: Cool and refrigerate leftover rice within one hour.Rice left at room temperature can grow harmful bacteria that cause food poisoning.
  • Warning: Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot all the way through,and never reheat them more than once.
